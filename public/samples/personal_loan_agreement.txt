PERSONAL LOAN FACILITY AGREEMENT

This Personal Loan Agreement is executed on 05/11/2024 between QuickRupee Finserve NBFC Limited ("Lender") and Mr. Amit S. Deshmukh ("Borrower"), residing at Thane West, Maharashtra.

LOAN SANCTION DETAILS:
1. Loan Principal Amount: ₹3,00,000/- (Rupees Three Lakhs only).
2. Loan Tenure: 36 (thirty-six) months.
3. Interest Rate: Floating rate of 18.5% per annum, calculated on monthly reducing balance, subject to unilateral revision by the Lender based on internal cost of funds.
4. Monthly Equated Installment (EMI): ₹10,928/- payable on the 5th day of each calendar month via NACH mandate.

CHARGES AND DEDUCTIONS:
5. Processing Fee: A non-refundable processing fee of 4.5% (₹13,500/-) plus 18% GST shall be deducted upfront from the sanctioned principal before disbursement.
6. Key Fact Statement (KFS): The Borrower acknowledges that a separate Key Fact Statement is dispensed with and this Agreement constitutes the entire disclosure.
7. Mandatory Loan Insurance: An amount of ₹7,800/- towards mandatory credit shield life insurance is debited from the disbursement proceeds. Net loan disbursed to borrower's account shall be ₹276,270/-.

PREPAYMENT AND FORECLOSURE:
8. Prepayment Penalty: If the Borrower repays the loan in part or forecloses the facility prior to maturity, a foreclosure penalty of 5% (five percent) plus applicable taxes on the outstanding principal balance shall be levied and payable by the Borrower.

DEFAULT, PENAL CHARGES AND RECOVERY:
9. Overdue Penal Interest: In the event of non-payment of any EMI on the due date, penal interest at the compounding rate of 3.0% per month (36% per annum) shall be levied on the entire overdue installment until realized in full.
10. Third-Party Debt Recovery: Upon default of 2 consecutive EMIs, the Lender is entitled to assign the debt or dispatch third-party collection agents to the Borrower's residence or employer premises without prior notice.
11. Data and Contact Consent: The Borrower gives unconditional consent to the Lender to access, share, and disclose the Borrower's KYC data, phone contact list, and repayment history with credit bureaus, social media platforms, and recovery partners.

DISPUTE RESOLUTION:
12. Exclusive jurisdiction for all recovery actions shall vest solely with the civil courts in Mumbai, Maharashtra.
