LEAVE AND LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Leave and License Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into at Pune, Maharashtra, on this 1st day of October, 2024 ("Execution Date"), by and between:

LICENSOR:
Mr. Rajesh V. Kulkarni, aged 54 years, residing at Bungalow No. 12, Prathamesh Park, Baner Road, Pune - 411045, Maharashtra (hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSOR", which expression shall include his legal heirs, executors, and assigns) of the ONE PART;

AND

LICENSEE:
Ms. Ananya Sharma, aged 28 years, presently residing at Flat 302, Green Meadows, Viman Nagar, Pune - 411014, Maharashtra (hereinafter referred to as the "LICENSEE", which expression shall include her legal heirs, executors, and permitted assigns) of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS the Licensor is the absolute owner of residential Flat No. 404, 4th Floor, 'B' Wing, Gulmohar Residency, Koregaon Park Annexe, Pune - 411001, admeasuring approximately 850 sq. ft. super built-up area (hereinafter referred to as the "Licensed Premises").

AND WHEREAS the Licensee has approached the Licensor to occupy the Licensed Premises purely on a temporary leave and license basis for residential dwelling purposes only for a period of 11 (eleven) months.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AND IT IS HEREBY MUTUALLY AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

1. TERM AND LICENSE:
The Licensor hereby grants unto the Licensee the temporary permission to use and occupy the Licensed Premises for residential purposes only for a fixed term of 11 (eleven) months commencing from 01/10/2024 and ending on 31/08/2025.

2. LICENSE FEE (RENT) AND ESCALATION:
The Licensee shall pay to the Licensor a monthly license fee of ₹28,000/- (Rupees Twenty-Eight Thousand only) per month, payable in advance on or before the 5th day of each calendar English month via electronic bank transfer. In the event this license is renewed or continued beyond 11 months, the monthly license fee shall automatically escalate by 10% (Ten Percent) per annum compounding annually.

3. INTEREST-FREE REFUNDABLE SECURITY DEPOSIT:
The Licensee has deposited with the Licensor an interest-free security deposit of ₹84,000/- (Rupees Eighty-Four Thousand only), equivalent to 3 (three) months' license fee. The Licensor shall retain the said deposit until vacant possession is returned. The Licensor reserves the unilateral right to deduct any damages, repainting charges, deep-cleaning charges, or utility arrears from the deposit at his sole discretion. No specific timeframe or deadline is provided for the refund of the balance deposit following the handover of keys.

4. LOCK-IN PERIOD AND TENANT EARLY TERMINATION PENALTY:
There shall be a strict lock-in period of 6 (six) months from 01/10/2024 to 31/03/2025. This lock-in period applies exclusively to the Licensee. If the Licensee vacates or issues notice to terminate the license before the completion of the 6-month lock-in period, the Licensee shall be liable to pay a mandatory penalty equivalent to 2 (two) months' license fee (₹56,000/-), and the security deposit shall stand forfeited.

5. NOTICE PERIOD AND TERMINATION:
After the expiry of the 6-month lock-in period:
(a) The Licensee may terminate this Agreement by serving a prior written notice of at least 60 (sixty) days to the Licensor.
(b) The Licensor reserves the overriding right to terminate this license agreement at any time by giving merely 15 (fifteen) days' written notice to the Licensee without assigning any reason whatsoever.

6. INSPECTION AND RIGHT OF ENTRY:
The Licensor or his designated property manager or relatives shall have the full and unrestricted right to enter upon and inspect the Licensed Premises at any time of the day or night without any prior notice or prior permission from the Licensee.

7. SOCIETY CHARGES AND UTILITIES:
The Licensee shall pay all electricity bills, piped cooking gas bills, internet charges, and monthly housing society maintenance charges of ₹3,500/- per month directly to the society office in addition to the monthly license fee.

8. USE, SUB-LETTING, AND RESTRICTIONS:
The Licensee shall not sub-let, assign, or part with possession of the premises to any third party. No commercial activities, pets, or overnight guests staying beyond 3 days are permitted without prior written consent.

9. FORMALITIES AND STAMP DUTY:
The parties acknowledge that this Agreement is executed privately between the parties. Neither party has purchased judicial stamp paper, and no registration before the Sub-Registrar of Assurances has been effected.

10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND ARBITRATION:
In the event of any dispute, difference, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, the same shall be referred to arbitration. The arbitration shall be conducted by a sole arbitrator appointed exclusively and unilaterally by the Licensor. The decision of such sole arbitrator shall be final and binding on the Licensee. The seat of arbitration shall be Pune.
