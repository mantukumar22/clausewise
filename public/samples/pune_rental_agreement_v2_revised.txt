The monthly license fee is agreed at ₹29,400/- per month (reflecting a negotiated 5% increase instead of 10%). If renewed for an additional period, escalation shall be capped at 5% (Five Percent) per annum.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT AND REFUND TIMELINE:

The Licensor shall refund the balance security deposit within 30 (thirty) days of the Licensee vacating the premises and handing over physical keys, subject only to actual itemized deductions for damages or unpaid utilities.

4. MUTUAL LOCK-IN PERIOD:
There shall be a mutual lock-in period of 6 (six) months from 01/09/2025 to 28/02/2026, binding on BOTH the Licensor and the Licensee equally. Neither party may terminate during this lock-in period without paying liquidated damages equal to 1 month's fee.

5. NOTICE PERIOD:
After the lock-in period, either party may terminate this Agreement by giving a symmetric 30 (thirty) days' written notice to the other party.

6. INSPECTION AND RIGHT OF ENTRY:
The Licensor may inspect the premises during reasonable daylight hours only after giving at least 24 (twenty-four) hours' prior written notice to the Licensee.

7. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL AND SPECIAL ESCALATION CLAUSE:
Unless the Licensee delivers a formal written refusal at least 45 days prior to expiry, this Agreement shall automatically renew for an additional consecutive term of 11 months with an immediate 12% (twelve percent) license fee hike.

8. E-STAMPING AND ONLINE REGISTRATION:
The parties agree that this Agreement shall be registered online under the Maharashtra e-Registration portal, with stamp duty and registration fees shared equally (50:50) between Licensor and Licensee.

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION:
Any dispute shall be referred to an independent sole arbitrator mutually agreed upon in writing by both parties under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996. Seat of arbitration shall be Pune.
